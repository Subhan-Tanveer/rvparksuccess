AUTOMATION RULES
================================================================================

PRE-ARRIVAL
─────────────────────────────────────
When:   72 hours before check-in
Status: Must be "confirmed" or "confirmed-deposit"
Disable: Per-park toggle in settings
Content: Check-in details, instructions, contact info

POST-STAY
─────────────────────────────────────
When:   24 hours after checkout
Status: Completed stay (not canceled)
Disable: Per-park toggle
Content: Thank you + review request + loyalty offer

RECOVERY
─────────────────────────────────────
When:   30-60 minutes before hold expires
Status: Reservation in "pending" state
Disable: Per-park toggle
Content: Abandoned cart + emergency discount

SEASONAL PROMO
─────────────────────────────────────
When:   Manual trigger via dashboard
Who:    All guests in park's history
Disable: Per-park toggle
Content: Custom offer, code, details
